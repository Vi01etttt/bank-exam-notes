## 一 考什么

| 项目 | 目前能确认的 | 依据 |
|---|---|---|
| 位置 | 第四单元“综合能力测试二（含英语）”，和逻辑推理、资料分析放在同一单元，是全场最后一个单元 | 2027年度统一笔试公告；2022、2023年笔经 |
| 题量 | 阅读理解约15题，通常3篇×5题 | 2022、2023年笔经都是15题；粉笔收录的2025年卷英语15题 |
| 时间 | 和逻辑、资料分析共用单元时间（往年约55分钟）；有机构说单独限时15分钟 | 笔经与高顿考情说法不一，以考场为准 |
| 难度 | 约六级，个别篇章偏难 | 高顿：中等偏难 |
| 题材 | 经济金融、商业管理为主，也有科技、社会新闻 | 高顿：金融经济方面考查较广 |

今年全场是14:30–17:00共150分钟，比2023年的14:30–17:30短半小时，单元内时间会更紧。四个单元各自计时，时间到自动跳到下一单元，**不能回头**。

## 二 明天的注意点

1. **留够英语时间。** 英语在最后一个单元，前面的资料分析最容易拖时间。给英语留至少15分钟（约1分钟1题）。如果单元内可以自由跳题，先做英语，再做逻辑，最后做资料分析。资料分析一道题卡住超过2分钟就先蒙一个，往后走。
2. **不留空。** 公告没有提到答错扣分。最后1分钟看到还有空着的题，全部选上同一个选项。
3. **先看题，再读文章。** 先扫5道题的题干，圈出人名、数字、年份、专有名词，再带着问题读文章。不要先把全文精读一遍。
4. **每题都回原文定位。** 选项必须在原文里找得到依据。凭印象或常识选，最容易掉进干扰项。
5. **不纠结生词。** 生词先看前后文有没有解释（that is、such as、破折号、括号），没解释就跳过。不影响做题的生词不要停下来猜。
6. **主旨题最后做。** 做完细节题，对文章的理解最完整，再做“main idea / best title”这类题。

## 三 五类题怎么做

| 题型 | 题干标志 | 做法 |
|---|---|---|
| 细节题 | According to the passage…；Which of the following is TRUE / NOT mentioned | 用题干关键词回原文定位，对比定位句和选项。正确答案通常是**同义替换**，原词照抄的选项反而要小心 |
| 主旨题 | main idea；best title；mainly discusses；purpose of the passage | 看首段和尾段，以及每段首句。选能概括全文的，排除只讲一段的、太宽泛的 |
| 推断题 | infer；imply；suggest；most likely | 答案在原文基础上只往前推一步。原文没有支持的“合理延伸”不选 |
| 词义题 | The word “…” in Paragraph 2 most probably means | 回原文看上下文，把选项代回句子读一遍。最常见的字面意思往往是陷阱 |
| 态度题 | author's attitude；tone | 找作者的评价词（形容词、副词）。考试文章作者很少极端，偏中性的选项更常对 |

### 3.1 干扰项的五个特征

| 特征 | 例子 | 判断 |
|---|---|---|
| 绝对化 | always、never、all、only、completely、must | 原文没有同样绝对的说法就排除 |
| 偷换主体 | 原文说“regulators”，选项说“banks” | 逐个核对主语 |
| 因果倒置或硬加因果 | 原文是同时发生，选项说A导致B | 原文要有 because、lead to、result in 等明确标志 |
| 程度或时态改变 | may → will；some → most；plan to → has done | 情态动词、数量词、时态逐个核对 |
| 原词堆砌 | 用原文原词拼出原文没有的意思 | 看起来最眼熟的选项最要警惕 |

## 四 今晚值得看的

### 4.1 信号词：看到就放慢

| 作用 | 词 | 读法 |
|---|---|---|
| 转折 | however、but、yet、nevertheless、nonetheless、although、while、whereas | 重点在转折后面，常考 |
| 因果 | because、since、therefore、thus、hence、consequently、as a result、lead to、result in、stem from | result from 和 result in 方向相反 |
| 递进 | moreover、furthermore、in addition、what's more | 前后观点一致 |
| 对比 | in contrast、on the contrary、unlike、compared with | 两个对象的区别常出细节题 |
| 举例 | for example、for instance、such as | 例子本身很少是答案，答案是例子前后的观点句 |
| 总结 | in short、in conclusion、overall、in a word | 主旨题的答案常在这里 |

### 4.2 态度词

| 正面 | 中性 | 负面 |
|---|---|---|
| optimistic 乐观的；supportive 支持的；favorable 赞成的；enthusiastic 热情的 | objective 客观的；neutral 中立的；cautious 谨慎的；ambivalent 矛盾的、喜忧参半的 | skeptical 怀疑的；critical 批评的；pessimistic 悲观的；indifferent 漠不关心的；hostile 敌对的 |

cautious（谨慎的）和 skeptical（怀疑的）最常被拿来做正确选项。indifferent 在态度题里几乎不会对：作者既然写这篇文章，就不会漠不关心。

### 4.5 常见同义替换

| 原文 | 选项里常写成 |

## 五 练习篇：限时6分钟

本篇为原创练习，不是建行原题。

> Banks around the world are racing to adopt generative artificial intelligence, but most are moving more slowly than the headlines suggest. A recent industry survey found that nearly nine in ten large banks are testing AI tools, yet fewer than a quarter have put them into use with real customers.
>
> The gap is not mainly about technology. Banks already use machine learning to detect fraud and to score credit. What makes generative AI different is that its answers cannot always be predicted or explained. A chatbot that gives a customer the wrong interest rate, for instance, could expose the bank to complaints and fines. Regulators have therefore asked banks to keep humans responsible for important decisions and to test models carefully before launch.
>
> As a result, most banks begin with internal tasks. Programmers use AI assistants to write and review code; staff use them to search policy documents and draft reports. These uses save time while keeping a person between the machine and the customer. Some banks report that routine coding work now takes about 30 percent less time.
>
> Critics argue that this caution could leave traditional lenders behind technology firms that move faster. Supporters reply that in banking, trust is the product. A bank that loses customers' trust through one serious error may take years to win it back. For now, the industry appears to have chosen to be a careful second rather than a reckless first.

**E1** What is the passage mainly about?

A. Why banks have stopped using machine learning
B. How banks are adopting generative AI with caution
C. How technology firms compete with traditional banks
D. Why regulators ban AI in customer services

**E2** According to the passage, what makes generative AI different from earlier machine learning tools?

A. It is much cheaper to build.
B. It can only be used for fraud detection.
C. Its outputs are not always predictable or explainable.
D. It does not need any human supervision.

**E3** Why do most banks begin with internal tasks?

A. Internal tasks are more profitable than customer services.
B. Regulators have banned all customer-facing AI.
C. Staff have refused to work with customers.
D. A person can check the results before they reach customers.

**E4** The word “reckless” in the last paragraph is closest in meaning to:

A. careless
B. successful
C. traditional
D. confident

**E5** What is the author's attitude toward banks' approach to AI?

A. Strongly critical
B. Indifferent
C. Generally understanding
D. Enthusiastic about faster adoption

### 答案与解析

| 题号 | 答案 | 解析 |
|---|---|---|
| E1 | B | 首段“racing to adopt… moving more slowly”，末段“careful second”，全文讲谨慎采用。A与原文相反（第二段说银行已在用机器学习）；C、D只是局部或原文没有的说法 |
| E2 | C | 第二段“its answers cannot always be predicted or explained”的同义替换。D与“keep humans responsible”相反 |
| E3 | D | 第三段“keeping a person between the machine and the customer”。B是绝对化：监管只是要求人负责、上线前测试，没有禁止 |
| E4 | A | reckless（鲁莽的）和前面的 careful 对比，意思是不顾后果、粗心。D是常见的“看起来通顺”的陷阱 |
| E5 | C | 作者先列批评者和支持者两方观点，最后说行业“appears to have chosen”谨慎路线，整体是理解、偏中性。A、D太极端；B不会对 |

做题顺序建议：先读5个题干，再读文章；E1放在最后做。
